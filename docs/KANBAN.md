## Scope and milestones

- **Sprints 1–2:** Original mission to aggregate/filter financial news, company disclosures and market data, identify trends and provide summaries/alerts. Documented; advanced news features need scope approval.
- **Sprint 3:** Portfolio-oriented WBS/schedule and risk management; holdings, transactions and allocation are earlier *candidate* ideas, not the Sprint 4–6 baseline.
- **Sprints 4–6:** Eight functional requirements FR-01–08, seven NFRs, user stories, Sprint 5 D1–D4 and Sprint 6 object/use-case/state/sequence/activity models.
- **Frontend:** Jake's sample Next.js UI is merged, with passing automated checks. It uses fictional prices, browser-only demo accounts/watchlists and local template explanations, not real APIs/AI.
- **Pending integration:** [Database design #30](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/30), [source/licensing #26](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/26), [AI provider #27](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/27), [secure login #44](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/44), [backend/frontend integration #46](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/46).
- **Expanded vision (proposed):** AI chat [#71](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/71), evidence checks [#72](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/72), company relationships [#73](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/73), holdings awareness [#74](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/74). Team scope, privacy and legal/financial-advice boundaries remain undecided.

## Board rules

Move a card by changing its single `stage:` label. Keep a feature Issue open until actual code, reviewed PR, tests, authorization and acceptance meet its criteria. A source model, demo screen or suggested design does not satisfy the production feature. Retain [scope blocker #25](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/25) and [Sprint 6 approval gate #68](https://github.com/JustEmre12/FinSight/issues/68) until actual decisions are recorded.
