# Project brief and scope

**Based on:** Sprint 1 proposal, Sprint 2 preliminary investigation, October 6 charter and the combined Sprints 4–5 requirements.

## Mission and users
FinSight is an AI-powered financial-market *research* platform for beginner and individual investors. Stock information is scattered across market data services, company information, news and social media; credibility, relevance and timeliness vary. FinSight aims to make research understandable and organized through search, data visualization, simple AI explanations, source references and personal watchlists.

## Minimum system boundary (combined Sprint 4/5 document)
- In scope: registration and login; stock search; current and historical market data; AI-generated market explanations; watchlists; stock comparison; charts; investment risk information.
- Out of scope: real trading, custody, linked bank accounts and transactions, automatically telling users to buy/sell, personalized fiduciary financial advice, guaranteed returns, or a dedicated native mobile app. Responsive mobile browsers **are** supported.
- Early *ideas* from Sprints 1–2: news/article aggregation, source ranking, trend identification, topic monitoring and personalized alerts. These are important original goals, but require explicit reconciliation with the later eight functional requirements.
- Sprint 3 portfolio tracking and the generic Fintech Data Tree introduce bank/transaction/goal ideas which are **not** automatically approved stock-research MVP features.

## Target interaction
A user searches a ticker or company, views dated price history and charts, compares stocks, requests a source-grounded, clearly labeled AI explanation, reads historical risk context, and (if logged in) saves companies to a watchlist. The user makes their own investment decisions.

## Current reality
The team has delivered *planning and analysis documents* through Sprint 5. None of the provided files demonstrates a running website, selected API vendor, deployed database, validated AI service, user study results or tested implementation. Engineering work is represented as open tracked tasks.
