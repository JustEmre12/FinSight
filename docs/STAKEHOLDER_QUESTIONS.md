# Ten questions for potential users

From Jake Pepitone's **FinSight - 10 Questions for Potential Users.docx**:

1. How do you track investments and portfolio performance today?
2. What would you want to see first on login?
3. Should FinSight cover stocks, ETFs, mutual funds or other asset types?
4. Is seeing total portfolio value and daily gain/loss important?
5. Would you record stock purchases/sales directly in FinSight?
6. Which holdings data matter: purchase/current price, gain/loss, share count?
7. Would major-price-change or portfolio-change alerts help?
8. Would stock/sector/asset allocation views be useful?
9. What would make you comfortable with stored financial information?
10. What would make you choose FinSight over a spreadsheet or tracker?

**Status:** Questions prepared; completed surveys/interviews and results not provided. Some questions describe portfolio tracking outside the later Sprint 4/5 baseline; use answers to guide scope decisions, not as finalized feature requirements.
