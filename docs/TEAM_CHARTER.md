# FinSight Team Charter (October 6, 2026)

**Source:** FinSight_Team_Charter.docx. A four-person BCS 301W team plans a usable AI-powered stock market research website.

| Member | Charter role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salih | Project Manager / Business Analyst | Milestones, meetings, requirements, task coordination |
| Jake | Frontend Developer / UI Designer | Screens, charts, dashboard, navigation, UX |
| Asad | Backend Developer / Database Manager | Backend, accounts, database and market APIs |
| Henry | AI / Data Analyst | Market data processing, trends, AI explanations |

**Important:** Roles other than Project Manager are proposed in the charter and may be adjusted by the team. Issues may suggest a role, but should not be treated as a GitHub assignment without account verification and consent.

## Operating norms
- Use group chat, respond to important messages within 24 hours.
- Meet at least weekly; report scheduling conflicts early.
- Divide tasks by role, skills and availability; record work honestly.
- Use GitHub version history, Issues and pull requests.
- Seek consensus, then majority vote; project manager breaks a tie.
- Try direct conversation, then group review, then PM mediation for conflicts; contact instructor if unresolved after 48 hours.
- Do not claim someone completed work without evidence.

## Boundaries
The charter explicitly excludes brokerage execution, personalized advice, guaranteed predictions, dedicated mobile app, and linked brokerage accounts. It includes browser-based market analysis and user-friendly charts.
